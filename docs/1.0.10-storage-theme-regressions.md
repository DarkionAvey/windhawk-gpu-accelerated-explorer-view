# Storage theme checks

Four individual regressions cover storage themes using private offscreen targets and private item identities. They do not access real drives or change focus, keyboard input or clipboard state.

- `theme-storage-heights`: independent fill/track heights in inline and tile layouts at 96, 120, 144 and 192 DPI, in LTR and RTL. The 8px/2px case must occupy exactly 10/3 physical pixels at 125% scaling, with no partially covered edge pixels. Odd/even physical heights may differ in center by exactly half a pixel; matching parity must align. It also checks the text-percentage/available-space cap, unchanged cell and row heights, tile text clearance, and zero-height drawing/resource short circuits without hiding the other part.
- `theme-storage-scale`: hard transitions at repeated percentages, channel interpolation, light/dark inside stops, separate appearance stop lists, accent interpolation, gradients, missing endpoints, invalid offsets and recovery of complete scale properties from malformed XML.
- `theme-storage-fill` and `theme-storage-track`: inside borders and patterns, color alpha multiplied by visibility fade, stretched and full-track gradients, physical gradient directions in RTL, warm cache stability, bounded dither storage, rounded corners, appearance changes, high-contrast/native fallback and device cleanup.

Expected physical heights come from independent DPI arithmetic. Pixel patterns and gradient colors are checked independently of the mod's geometry, stroke-style and interpolation helpers. These checks preserve the requested crisp sizes instead of changing their parity to force exact center alignment. They are component checks, not live Explorer frame-rate or presentation measurements.
