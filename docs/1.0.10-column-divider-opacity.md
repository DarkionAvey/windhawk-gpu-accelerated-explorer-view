# Details column divider contrast

The supplied screenshot has a background of RGB (30, 30, 30) behind the Details header. At rows 20, 40 and 55, its internal vertical dividers are RGB (55, 55, 55), while the main native navigation-pane separator stroke is RGB (42, 42, 42). The native separator also has neighboring edge strokes, so matching its main stroke does not reproduce its entire geometry.

The mod used the native header text color at 14% opacity for both internal dividers and the outer vertical rule beside the scrollbar. Keeping that theme color and reducing its contribution by `(42−30)/(55−30)` gives approximately 6.72% opacity. Both paths now share a 1/15 (6.67%) opacity. No fixed RGB fill or background sampling was added to rendering.

This is a contrast match measured from the supplied dark-mode screenshot. The foreground still follows the native theme and blends over the actual Explorer background. A corresponding light-mode capture is needed to verify the visual match there. Header text, the horizontal bottom rule, geometry, hit targets and animation fading retain their existing behavior.
